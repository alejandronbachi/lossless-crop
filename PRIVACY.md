# Privacy Policy for Lossless Crop

This Privacy Policy governs the data practices of the Lossless Crop application.

## 1. Local Processing
Lossless Crop operates primarily as a local desktop application. Image cropping, format adjustments, and metadata preservation (Exif and ICC profiles) are executed strictly within your local machine's system memory and hard drive. No images are ever transmitted to an external server.

## 2. Optional Manual Data Transmission (Feedback & Support)
The application does not automatically collect or transmit any data. Data transmission only occurs when a user explicitly initiates a support or feedback request through the designated menu option:
* **Feedback Delivery:** Messages are sent securely via an HTTP POST request to FormSubmit (a third-party form-handling service) to deliver your feedback to the developer.
* **Anonymity & Email Data:** Submissions are entirely anonymous by default. However, users have the optional choice to provide their personal email address if they wish to receive a direct reply regarding their inquiry. This email address is used solely for support communication and is never shared or sold.

## 3. Contact Support
If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the official GitHub repository: [https://github.com](https://github.com/alejandronbachi/lossless-crop)
